# Bounty Hold'em

Texas Hold'em for your party, paid for by the mobs you kill.

Every mob you kill drops chips into your pot. Get your group together, open a table and play them.

## How to play

1. **Earn chips.** Just play the game. Normal mobs pay 1 chip, elites 5, rares 25, rare elites 30 and bosses 100. Grey mobs pay nothing. You can earn up to 1,000 a day, and new characters start with 200.
2. **Get a group.** One of you types `/poker host`. Everyone else in the party or raid gets a pop-up asking if they want to play.
3. **Sit down.** Click Play, then click an empty seat. Your chips come with you.
4. **Deal.** The host presses Deal Hand (or ticks Auto-deal) and you play normal Texas Hold'em: blinds, check, call, raise, fold, best five cards wins.

Short on players? The host can fill empty seats with bots.

## The chip pot

The little stack of chips on your screen is your balance. It bounces every time a kill pays out. Open poker once a day for a free 100-chip bonus.

- **Left-click** opens the table.
- **Right-click** shows a leaderboard of your characters and anyone you've played with.
- **Drag** to move it.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/poker` | Open the table |
| `/poker host` | Host a table for your group |
| `/poker start` / `pause` | Start or pause dealing |
| `/poker blinds`, `timer`, `kick`, `void`, `close` | Host controls |
| `/poker bot [n]`, `/poker nobots` | Add or remove bots |
| `/poker chips` | Your balance and today's earnings |
| `/poker pot` | Show or hide the chip pot |

## Good to know

- Everyone at the table needs the addon.
- Chips are just for fun. They have no gold value and can't be traded.
- Balances are kept on your own PC, so it runs on trust.
